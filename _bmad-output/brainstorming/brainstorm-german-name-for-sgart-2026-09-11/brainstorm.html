Technique 8 · mini-round

Einkaufsfuchs Feinschliff

Spelling, claim, and icon-shortform for the name the household actually picked

Decision Chosen direction: Einkaufsfuchs over Bonfuchs. Right now, and across the roadmap, the core function is shopping-list management and store-grouped planning — receipt scanning and price history are a later enhancement on top of that core, not the anchor. The name leads with Einkauf, not Bon.
Insight Einkaufsfuchs wasn't in the original shortlist verbatim, but it's the natural next generation of the Fuchs family from Round Robin (Fuchskorb, Vorratsfuchs, Kühlfuchs, Schlaufuchs) — and it quietly recreates the original SGART wordplay in German: Einkauf = Grocery, Fuchs (schlau wie ein Fuchs) = Smart. The most literal German echo of "Smart Grocery" the whole session produced.

Schreibweise — spelling variants

Einkaufsfuchsfull, formal spelling

Einkaufs-Fuchshyphenated — easier to parse at small sizes
Kauffuchscontraction dropping "eins" — shorter and punchier, still reads clearly as Einkauf+Fuchs

Claim — tagline options

„Schlau einkaufen, gemeinsam.“brings back the Fuchs=schlau wordplay explicitly; emphasizes the shared-household angle
„Weiß, wo's günstig ist.“personifies the fox; teases the future price feature without promising it as a core function
„Die Einkaufsliste, die mitdenkt.“foregrounds the smart list — today's core function — over the price angle
„Nie mehr ohne Butter an der Kasse.“pulled straight from PITCH.md's opening hook; concrete and relatable
„Einer für alle, eine Liste für alle.“Three-Musketeers wordplay, underscoring shared household use
„Schlau geplant ist halb gekauft.“twist on "well planned is half won" — feels like an existing proverb, the same trick Schlaufuchs uses

Icon-Kurzform — short-label options

Fuxplayful short spelling of Fuchs, reused from the Crank-the-Dial round
EFplain initials — clean but generic/cold, listed as the contrast option
Fuxidiminutive, very cute/friendly — maybe too playful for the later cost-overview positioning
Fuchs-Piktogramma fox pictogram as the actual app icon, with Einkaufsfuchs as the wordmark beside it
Kauffuxmashup of the Kauffuchs contraction and the Fux spelling — fits as a home-screen label under the icon
Insight Einkaufsfuchs is likely too long as a home-screen label under the app icon — iOS/Android truncate past a certain length. Kauffux or Fux should take that slot, while Einkaufsfuchs stays the full brand for the store listing and website.

✨ Final Decision ✨

The Fox Wins

Seven techniques, one mini-round, one name for the household

🦊 Einkaufsfuchs

Seven techniques surfaced Hamster, Zettel, Ping, and a proverb clash along the way — in the end, the Fuchs family carried the day.

Chosen over Bonfuchs: the core function now and on the roadmap is shopping-list management and store-grouped planning, not receipt or price tracking — so the name leads with Einkauf, not Bon. It also happens to be the cleanest German echo of the app's own origin: Einkauf = Grocery, Fuchs (schlau wie ein Fuchs) = Smart.
