- (insight) SYNTHESIS: Hamster (hamstern=to stockpile) is the strongest independently-converged root, found via round robin, evolutionary breeding, onomatopoeia, and the Germanness dial. Ping reuses the pitch's own literal word for its privacy-preserving notification. The proverb clash (was ich nicht weiss, macht mich nicht heiss) is tagline material, not a name. Two rival strategies emerged: reused-idiom names (Schlaufuchs, Pfennigfuchser - warm, less ownable) vs invented/blended words (Kassaro, Fux - brandable, colder) - this is the marketing-vs-mom tension from Role Playing. Fux could serve as a short brand token/wordmark independent of whichever full name wins. Household-safe names (Wieselkiste, Bibertresor) survived evolutionary selection specifically for not reading as solo-hoarding, which matters since SGART is shared/household-first. Shortlist to carry forward: Hamsterbon, Bonfuchs (seed), Wieselkiste, Bibertresor, Fuechslein/Fux, Kritzelliste.
