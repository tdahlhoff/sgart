All three keep names at full width (status costs no horizontal space). A tints the whole row — boldest, fastest to scan, busiest when the list is long. B keeps rows white with a thin colored edge bar — calmest, most in line with the „flat, ruhig" tone. C is a filled tinted tag on a second line — visible, but the row is taller. Same four semantic colors throughout: Admin · Mitglied · Ausstehend · Kette.
