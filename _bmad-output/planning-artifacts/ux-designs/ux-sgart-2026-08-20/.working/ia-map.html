SGART · UX · Information Architecture

App-Karte — MVP-Struktur

Three bottom-nav tabs, a persistent household switcher, and household management kept in household context. Every node is tagged with the SPEC capability it serves — all fourteen (CAP-1…14) have a home. Rendered in the decided design language (Inter, locked palette, System 3).
